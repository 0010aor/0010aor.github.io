      About Curvewise

      Privacy

      Curvewise runs entirely in your browser. Images you open are decoded and converted on your device; your image is never sent to a server, stored, or shared. To see how much the tool is used, we count page visits and finished conversions anonymously, without cookies or personal data. Error details are only shared if you copy and send them yourself.

      Tips

      • Use the largest, cleanest version of your image you have. Blurry or heavily compressed images give lumpier shapes.
      • If small specks appear, lower the detail. If thin parts disappear, raise it.
      • For a logo with a known number of colours, pick that number under Colours.
